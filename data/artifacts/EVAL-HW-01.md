## Question 1

What is 47 x 23?

**Given:** 47 × 23

**Steps:**
1. Break down 23 into 20 + 3
2. 47 × 3 = 141
3. 47 × 20 = 940
4. Add the partial products: 141 + 940 = 1081

**Answer:** 47 × 23 = 1081
