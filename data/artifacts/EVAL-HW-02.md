## Question 1

Solve for x: 3x + 7 = 22

**Given:** 3x + 7 = 22

**Steps:**
1. Start with the equation: 3x + 7 = 22
2. Subtract 7 from both sides: 3x + 7 − 7 = 22 − 7 → 3x = 15
3. Divide both sides by 3: x = 15 ÷ 3
4. x = 5
5. Verification: 3(5) + 7 = 15 + 7 = 22 ✓

**Answer:** x = 5
