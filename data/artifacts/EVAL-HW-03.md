# Train speed unit conversion

_Subject:_ **physics** &middot; _Date:_ 2026-08-20 &middot; _Solved by Beepsme_

## Question 1

A train travels 60 km in 45 minutes. Speed in km/h?

**Given:** Distance = 60 km, Time = 45 minutes = 45/60 h = 0.75 h

**Steps:**
1. Convert time to hours: 45 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours
2. Apply speed formula: Speed = Distance ÷ Time
3. Speed = 60 km ÷ 0.75 h
4. Speed = 80 km/h

**Answer:** 80 km/h
