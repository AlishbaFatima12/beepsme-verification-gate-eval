## Question 1

If 5 pens cost 75 rupees, how much do 8 pens cost?

**Given:** 5 pens = 75 rupees; find cost of 8 pens

**Steps:**
1. Step 1: Find the cost of 1 pen. Cost of 1 pen = 75 ÷ 5 = 15 rupees
2. Step 2: Find the cost of 8 pens. Cost of 8 pens = 15 × 8 = 120 rupees

**Answer:** 8 pens cost 120 rupees.
